Theses

What I believe will change in the next few years

Each thesis is something I believe, and what to do about it today. Written down on 30 October 2026. On 30 October 2027 I’ll check them and add the result here.

  • Buildings become batteries

    Heat is stored in the hours when power is cheap instead of being produced when it’s expensive. District heating and large buildings start working like a city’s battery.

    What to do nowIf you’re renewing your heating, plan for storage now.

    I’ve worked on energy from both ends: the technology and the policy.

    Utilitas heat storage at Väo, Tallinn: 1,100 MWh · Utilitas

    1 Jan 2028 ETS2: carbon price on fuels for buildings and road transport · Reg (EU) 2026/667

  • Agents get a mandate, not a password

    People and companies give software limited rights, like a power of attorney: what it may do, how much, and for how long.

    What to do nowBefore you deploy an agent, write down its limits.

    I’m mapping who answers when an AI agent gets it wrong.

    9 Dec 2026 Product liability rules start to cover software, including AI · Directive (EU) 2024/2853

  • Trust becomes measurable

    The winner is whoever can show the customer what their system did and why, not whoever promises the most.

    What to do nowAsk your supplier what they can show you about their system’s decisions.

    I built a readiness check that takes its dates from the law, not from the model.

    2 Dec 2027 AI Act: high-risk systems (Annex III) · Reg (EU) 2026/1744
