About

From ministry rules to working tools

I worked as a climate and renewable energy specialist at Estonia’s Ministry of Regional Affairs and Agriculture. My work covered the Climate Act, the national energy and climate plan (REKK 2030, the NECP), biomethane rules and EU working groups. What I took with me is a feel for what comes next and when, especially how Estonia writes an EU rule into its own law and what changes along the way. I also learned that an obligation always reaches a company later, and messier, than it was written in Brussels.

I started building AI tools when I saw models answer regulatory questions with confidence and get them wrong. I asked three models about the AI Act deadlines, and all three gave me the same wrong answer. I wanted to see whether I could build something whose answer stays tied to the original source. That is how the AI Act Readiness Check and the agent liability map came about.

On my radar

What’s becoming possible

Clean power keeps getting cheaper to produce, and AI can already take on real work. Rules mostly decide the timing, not the direction. These are the openings I follow.

  • Heat from cheap, clean power

    When wind and sun produce more than is needed, power gets cheap. Heat pumps, electric boilers and heat storage can use those hours to keep homes and towns warm. A carbon price on heating fuels, coming with ETS2, pushes the same way.

    I’ve worked on energy from both ends: the technology and the policy.

    1 Jan 2028 ETS2: carbon price on fuels for buildings and road transport · Reg (EU) 2026/667

  • Farms that also make energy

    Manure and crop residues can become biomethane: local fuel, lower emissions and a second income for the farm.

    I come from agriculture and worked on biomethane at the ministry.

  • Small teams with AI agents

    AI agents can already read documents, data and rules and do work that used to need a whole department.

    I built a readiness check that takes these dates from the law, not from the model.

    2 Dec 2027 AI Act: high-risk systems (Annex III) · Reg (EU) 2026/1744

  • Agents people can trust

    AI agents get real work only when people can trust them. That takes evidence: what the agent was allowed to do, and what it actually did.

    I’m mapping who answers when an AI agent gets it wrong.

    9 Dec 2026 Product liability rules start to cover software, including AI · Directive (EU) 2024/2853

Töötasin Regionaal- ja Põllumajandusministeeriumis kliima- ja taastuvenergia spetsialistina. Tegelesin kliimaseaduse, REKK 2030, NECP-i ja biometaani regulatsiooniga ning osalesin EL-i töögruppides. Minu radaril

Mis muutub võimalikuks

Puhta elektri tootmine läheb aina odavamaks ja AI suudab juba päris tööd teha. Reeglid otsustavad enamasti ajastuse, mitte suuna. Need on võimalused, mida ma jälgin.

  • Soojus odavast puhtast elektrist

    Kui tuul ja päike toodavad rohkem, kui parasjagu vaja, läheb elekter odavaks. Soojuspumbad, elektrikatlad ja soojussalvestid saavad neid tunde kasutada, et kodud ja linnad oleksid soojad. Kütuste süsinikuhind, mis tuleb ETS2-ga, lükkab samas suunas.

    Olen energiaga tegelenud mõlemast otsast: tehnika ja poliitika poolelt.

    1. jaan 2028 ETS2: hoonete ja maanteetranspordi kütuste süsinikuhind · määrus (EL) 2026/667

  • Talud, mis toodavad ka energiat

    Sõnnikust ja taimejäätmetest saab biometaani: kohalik kütus, väiksem heide ja talule teine sissetulek.

    Tulen põllumajandusest ja tegelesin biometaaniga ministeeriumis.

  • Väikesed meeskonnad AI-agentidega

    AI-agendid suudavad juba lugeda dokumente, andmeid ja reegleid ning teha tööd, milleks varem oli vaja tervet osakonda.

    Ehitasin valmisoleku kontrolli, mis võtab need kuupäevad seadusest, mitte mudelist.

    2. dets 2027 AI Act: suure riskiga süsteemid (III lisa) · määrus (EL) 2026/1744

  • Agendid, mida julgeb usaldada

    AI-agendid saavad päris töö alles siis, kui neid julgetakse usaldada. Selleks on vaja tõendit: mida agent tohtis teha ja mida ta tegelikult tegi.

    Kaardistan, kes vastutab, kui AI-agent eksib.

    9. dets 2026 Tootevastutus hakkab katma ka tarkvara, sh AI-d · direktiiv (EL) 2024/2853
