EU AI Act Readiness Check

I asked three models about the AI Act deadlines, and all three gave the same wrong answer. This tool tells you what the AI Act means for your system, and by when.

Why you can trust itThe model never writes a date. It picks a category, and code adds the date and the legal source. Test cases check the result. Every month a scheduled agent checks whether a new act has changed the dates.

Note · 30 October 2026

Energy agents need a receipt

What got cheaper
Software that decides on its own when to heat or charge a battery. Homes and companies already use it.
What it makes possible
Selling flexibility to the grid without anyone pressing a button. Companies already do it through aggregators. The joint Baltic balancing market started in February 2025. Elering
What still holds it back
Trust. There is no receipt: what the agent was allowed to do, what it did and what came of it. From 9 December 2026, software comes under product liability. Directive (EU) 2024/2853

Küsisin kolmelt mudelilt AI Acti tähtaegade kohta ja kõik kolm andsid sama vale vastuse. See tööriist ütleb, mida AI Act sinu süsteemile tähendab ja mis ajaks.
