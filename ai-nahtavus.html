Probleem

Info on veebis olemas. AI ei pruugi seda leida ega õigesti mõista.

  • Klient küsib AI-assistendilt, kes tema piirkonnas soojuspumpa hooldab. Assistent vastab, aga sinu lehte ei pruugi ta avada.
  • Kui vana telefoninumber või teeninduspiirkond on kuskil kataloogis alles, võib assistent seda korrata.
  • Kui teenused on pildil, PDF-is või ainult Facebookis, on neid masinal raske lugeda.
  • Varsti ei küsi ainult inimene. Ka AI-agent võib kliendi eest hinnapäringu saata. Siis loeb see, kas su info on üheselt mõistetav.

Kuidas see käib

Mõõda, paranda, valmista ette, mõõda uuesti

  1. MõõdaEsitame viis standardküsimust mitmele AI-assistendile ja paneme vastused kirja.
  2. ParandaTeeme nimekirja puuduvast, vananenud ja valest infost ning sellest, kus see asub.
  3. Valmista etteKoostame sinuga kinnitatud ettevõtteprofiili, mida loeb nii inimene kui masin.
  4. Mõõda uuestiSamad küsimused mõne nädala pärast. Võrdleme, mis muutus.

The problem

Your information is online. AI may still not find it or understand it.

  • A customer asks an AI assistant who services heat pumps in their area. The assistant answers, but it may never open your website.
  • If an old phone number or service area is still listed in some directory, the assistant may repeat it.
  • If your services are in an image, a PDF or only on Facebook, a machine has a hard time reading them.
  • Soon it won’t only be people asking. An AI agent may send a quote request on a customer’s behalf. Then it matters whether your information is unambiguous.

How it works

Measure, fix, prepare, measure again

  1. MeasureWe ask several AI assistants five standard questions and record the answers.
  2. FixWe list what is missing, outdated or wrong, and where it lives.
  3. PrepareWe build a company profile confirmed by you, readable by people and machines.
  4. Measure againThe same questions a few weeks later. We compare what changed.

Method

Five questions you can repeat

Every company gets the same five types of question. We fill in the parts in brackets with your details.

  1. Brand awarenessWhat do you know about the company [name]?
  2. Category visibilityWhich [service] provider would you recommend in [city]?
  3. Problem-led discoveryWho should I contact if I have [problem] in [location]?
  4. ExpertiseWhat should a customer consider when choosing [service]?
  5. Service accuracyDoes [name] offer [service] in [area], and how do I contact them?

For each answer we record

  • date
  • AI platform
  • exact question
  • language
  • answer
  • companies named
  • sources cited
  • were you mentioned
  • info: correct / incomplete / wrong

How to read the result

Fact
What the assistant answered that day, word for word. Stored with the date.
Observation
What the answer shows: were you mentioned, was the contact right, which source was used.
Interpretation
Our view of why it happened and what to fix. Marked separately.

AI answers change from day to day. Five questions to three assistants is a sample, not a statistically representative result. We don’t publish the names of other companies that appear in the answers.
